2. What the Service is

NEURL-OS asks you a set of questions once a week about sleep, workload, movement, stress, attention and fuel. It applies a scoring model to your answers and returns a score, a projection of where that score is trending, and suggested areas of focus.

The score is generated by a deterministic weighted model that combines your current answers with the pattern of your previous weeks. It reflects what you reported. It is a structured way to look at your own self-assessment — not a measurement taken from your body, and not a clinical instrument.
