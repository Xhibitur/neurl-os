2. What we collect

Information that stays on your device

When you take the daily reaction test, your results (reaction times, response speed, slowest taps, consistency, lapses and errors) and your NEURL Score history are saved in your browser's local storage on the device you used. If you rate how alert you feel before a test, or answer the optional questions after it (your wake time, sleep, movement, late screens, workload and alcohol), those answers are stored the same way, along with the habit metrics and patterns calculated from them.

This data is not uploaded to us. It does not leave your device unless you take one of the specific actions described below. If you clear your browser data or use a different device, that history is gone and we cannot restore it.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit, which screens they reach, how long they stay, whether they come back, and how many complete a test. It only loads if you tap Accept on our cookie banner. It records a truncated IP address, approximate region, device type and browser. We send counts such as "test completed" or "habit questions answered", your score as a number, whether you are logged in, and your age range if you added one. We never send your habit answers, including alcohol, to Google Analytics.
  • Duel links. When you challenge a friend, the link holds only the first name you choose to add (optional) and your NEURL Score. Anyone you send it to can see both. Duel results and badges are saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • Teams and club boards. If you start or join a team, or join a club board, we store a random device ID, the nickname you choose and your weekly numbers (tests taken, average NEURL Score, change from last week, streak, and whether your 7-day baseline is done). Other members of that team or club board can see your nickname and those numbers. Teams also appear on the public Top Teams list by team name, size and weekly totals only, unless the team creator turns that off. We never share your email, habit answers or alcohol answers there. Leaving a team deletes your numbers from it.
  • Partner attribution. If you arrive through a partner link (for example a gym's QR code), we record which partner sent you, so we can tell that partner how many people their link reached. This is reported to partners only as totals, never as a list of individuals.
  • Local storage. Beyond your test history, we store small technical values such as your cookie choice, your reminder preference and which partner page you arrived from, so the experience stays consistent between visits.

3. How partner versions protect you

Gyms, studios and companies can offer a branded version of NEURL-OS to their members or employees. These versions are built so that no individual's data is exposed:

  • Partners receive totals only, such as how many people opened their link or took a test. They never receive your scores, your habit answers or your email address from us.
  • Any group figure is only shown when it is drawn from at least five people. Below that, nothing is shown, because a figure drawn from a very small group can identify the people in it.
  • Alcohol answers are never included in anything a partner sees, in any form.
  • There are no leaderboards or rankings. If you use NEURL-OS through an employer, your manager cannot see your results.
  • Age comparisons are anonymous and are only shown for groups of at least 30 people.

4. How we use information

  • To calculate and display your score, habit metrics, patterns and weekly focus
  • To send reminders and updates you have asked to receive
  • To give partners aggregate totals, subject to the protections above

  • To report aggregate reach to partners who refer people to us
  • To meet legal obligations and to protect the service from abuse

5. We do not sell your information

Xhibitur LLC does not sell personal information, and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months.

We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers who run the infrastructure on our behalf — our hosting and storage provider (Netlify), and Google Analytics. They may process information only to provide their service to us.
  • Partners receive totals only, as described in section 3.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, valid legal process, or to protect rights, safety or the integrity of the service.
  • A business transfer — if Xhibitur LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction. We will post notice here before your information becomes subject to a different policy.

7. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Delete that information
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Stop sending you email or push notifications

Email info@neurl-os.com and we will respond within 45 days. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

California residents

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act gives you the rights to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing of personal information. As stated in section 5, we do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of — but you may still exercise your other rights using the contact above. You may also designate an authorised agent to make a request for you.

Turning things off yourself

  • Analytics: tap Decline on the cookie banner, or change your choice any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. We also honor Global Privacy Control signals. You can also install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Push notifications: turn them off in your browser's site settings for neurl-os.com.
  • Everything on your device: clearing site data for neurl-os.com erases your entire local history.

8. Cookies and similar technology

We use two categories:

  • Strictly necessary — local storage that holds your test history, habit answers, profile (if you log in), cookie choice, reminder and calendar settings, and partner context. The product does not work without these.
  • Analytics — Google Analytics cookies (named _ga and _ga_*), which load only after you tap Accept. If you later decline, we stop analytics and delete these cookies.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not allow third-party advertising networks on this site.

9. Security

The site is served entirely over HTTPS. Administrative functions are protected by a credential verified on the server, never in the browser. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — but the design deliberately minimises what we hold in the first place, which is the strongest protection available.
